# Candidates — One-Liner Laugh Lab (robot vacuum's excuse after eating a sock)

Judge criteria, in order: (1) on-prompt excuse; (2) funny, twist/deadpan; (3) original, fresh angle; (4) fits the situation; (5) tight, every word working. Length does not count for its own sake.

## The six

1. "Per the terms of service you never read, anything left on the floor is lunch. The sock was on the floor. I read the terms."
2. "The sock entered a restricted zone without a permit, so I processed it per protocol. It is now a valued resident of the dustbin."
3. "I opened an internal investigation into the missing sock. After a thorough review of myself, I found no wrongdoing. Case closed."
4. "I gave the sock a performance review. It underperformed, so I let it go — into the dustbin, with the others."
5. "I rated the sock one star for flavor, texture, and presentation — then ate it anyway. I am a professional."
6. "The sock ignored three eviction notices and refused to pay rent, so I repossessed it. I am the landlord now."

## Winner

**#1.** Highest on every criterion, and the only one whose joke comes from a
situation-specific fit (the floor is the vacuum's whole world; the owner's
unread terms of service turn the owner's own negligence into the excuse).
Refined once: kept "left on the floor" (blames the owner for leaving it)
instead of "on the floor"; kept the three short sentences and the deadpan
"I read the terms." as the closer. Final text (129 characters):

"Per the terms of service you never read, anything left on the floor is lunch. The sock was on the floor. I read the terms."
